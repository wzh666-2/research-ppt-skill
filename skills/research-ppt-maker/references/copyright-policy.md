# Copyright and provenance policy

1. Do not copy a supplied template, master, exact layout coordinates, theme,
   icon set, images, wording, or sample data.
2. Treat private references as read-only research material and never add them or
   their extracted contents to the repository or output bundle.
3. Prefer editable original redraws based on cited numerical values.
4. Embed a paper image only when `image.explicitPermission` is `true` and the
   image records `source`, `license`, and `riskNote`.
5. If license information is absent or ambiguous, do not embed the image. Add a
   visible missing-input marker instead.
6. Synthetic examples must be plainly identified as synthetic on the relevant
   slides and in the source register.
7. Record user-supplied assets as such; do not claim authorship over them.

Technical checks reduce risk but cannot provide an absolute legal guarantee.
